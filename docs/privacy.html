Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

Overview

AskFuji is a camera companion app for the Fuji X100VI. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected when you use AskFuji and how it is used.

Information We Collect

AskFuji does not require you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information.

When you submit a question in the chat, your message is sent to our server and forwarded to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response. Questions are not stored by AskFuji after a response is returned.

Anthropic / Claude API

Chat questions are processed by Anthropic's Claude API. Anthropic may retain messages in accordance with their own privacy policy. You can review Anthropic's privacy practices at anthropic.com/privacy.

Local Storage

AskFuji stores your tip history locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

Push Notifications

If you grant permission, AskFuji sends a daily tip notification at 8:00 AM. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No personal data is transmitted to enable notifications.

Third-Party Services

AskFuji uses the following third-party services:

Anthropic Claude API — processes your chat questions. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

Vercel — hosts our API server. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

AskFuji is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact
